How to report responsibly

Help the community by submitting factual, verifiable information.

01. Include evidence

Include relevant dates, public links, agreements and transaction evidence with private details hidden.

02. Protect privacy

Never upload passwords, identity documents, bank account details or unrelated people's private information.

03. Fair review

Reports should be reviewed before publication. Give the subject an opportunity to respond when appropriate.

04. Verify licences

A company not appearing in one search is not automatically proof of fraud. Confirm its exact legal name and jurisdiction.

Reports are allegations until independently verified. Do not use this website to harass or threaten anyone.
